02 LLM alignment and post-training ICML 2025

The shape of a reward changes what a model learns.

AlphaPO introduces a tunable reward shape for preference optimization, controlling likelihood displacement and over-optimization.

Why it matters. More control over alignment, with improvements over SimPO and DPO on the evaluated Mistral-7B and Llama-3-8B models.

03 Efficient foundation models EMNLP 2025

Make the model smaller. Keep what matters.

We combine knowledge distillation, structured pruning, and quantization to compress language models for recommendation workloads at LinkedIn.

Why it matters. Retain much of the larger model’s quality while reducing training and serving cost and latency, with lessons from deployment.

Scaling Down, Serving Fast: Compressing and Deploying Efficient LLMs for Recommendation Systems
