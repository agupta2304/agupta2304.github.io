About

I lead company-wide research on AI agents, foundation models, and LLMs at Nubank, where the systems we build serve more than 135 million customers. My work sits at the intersection of large-scale optimization, deep learning, and language models, much of it in long-running collaborations with Keerthi Selvaraj, Rahul Mazumder, and Natesh Pillai.

Before Nubank I spent six years at LinkedIn, most recently as a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer and Senior Manager in Core AI, where I led the team compressing and distilling LinkedIn's ranking foundation model and drove large-scale optimization across Feed, Ads, and Jobs. Earlier I was a Senior Machine Learning Research Scientist at Apple and a software engineer at Amazon. I studied at Carnegie Mellon and BITS Pilani.

Featured research

Agents in production

Getting agents past a demo means being able to say, with evidence, that they will behave on the next hundred million conversations. I work on evaluation harnesses rigorous enough to gate a deployment, and on the failure modes that only appear once real customers are on the other end.

  • KDD 2026 Building Customer Support AI Agents at 100M-User Scale: An Evaluation-Driven Framework
  • Tutorial @ CIKM 2026 Building Production LLM Agents: An Evaluation-Driven Playbookhalf-day tutorial · Nov 2026
  • Talk @ AI Engineer SimulationMaxxing: How we ship agents 20× fasterwith Shreya Rajpal · Jul 2026

Alignment and post-training

Preference optimization is usually studied through its objective, but the shape of the reward and the robustness of the reward model matter just as much. I look at where the standard recipes are brittle and what to change so alignment holds up outside the benchmark.

  • ICML 2025 AlphaPO: Reward Shape Matters for LLM Alignment
  • ICML 2025 On the Robustness of Reward Models for Language Model Alignment

Efficiency at scale

A model that cannot be served is a result, not a product. This thread covers compressing and distilling large models down to a serving budget, quantizing optimizer state so training fits, and scheduling inference so latency targets survive real traffic.

  • EMNLP 2025 Scaling Down, Serving Fast: Compressing and Deploying Efficient LLMs for Recommendation Systems
  • SPOT@ICLR 2026 Effective Quantization of Muon Optimizer States
  • NeurIPS 2025 LLM Query Scheduling with Prefix Reuse and Latency Constraints

Recommender systems at scale

I have worked across the recommender stack: retrieval, ranking, personalization, and the large-scale constrained optimization that sits on top of it. That last part meant solving linear programs with trillions of variables.

  • Tutorial @ KDD 2023 Practical Design of Performant Recommender Systems using Large-scale Linear Programming-based Global Inference
  • WWW 2025 Efficient Algorithms for Leveraging LLMs for Generative and Predictive Recommender Systems
  • KDD 2024 LiRank: Industrial Large Scale Ranking Models at LinkedIn
  • KDD 2022 Generalized Deep Mixed Models
