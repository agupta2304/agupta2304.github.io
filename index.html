About

I lead company-wide research on AI agents, foundation models, and LLMs at Nubank, where the systems we build serve more than 100 million customers.

My work sits at the intersection of large-scale optimization, deep learning, and language models. Lately that has meant alignment and post-training, efficient training and inference, and the surprisingly hard problem of evaluating agents rigorously enough to put them in front of real customers. I care about research that survives contact with production.

Before Nubank I spent six years at LinkedIn, most recently as a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer and Senior Manager in Core AI, where I led the team compressing and distilling LinkedIn's ranking foundation model and drove large-scale optimization across Feed, Ads, and Jobs. Earlier I was a Senior Machine Learning Research Scientist at Apple and a software engineer at Amazon. I studied at Carnegie Mellon and BITS Pilani.

I have published across ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, WWW, CIKM, and EMNLP, filed more than ten patents, and serve on the program committees for ICLR, KDD, WWW, CIKM, and RecSys. I am always happy to hear from people who want to work on these problems — LinkedIn is the fastest way to reach me.
