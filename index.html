About

I lead research on LLM training and agents at Nubank, building and evaluating large-scale AI systems that serve financial services to well over 100 million customers.

My work sits at the intersection of large-scale optimization, deep learning, and language models. Lately that has meant alignment and post-training, efficient training and inference, and the surprisingly hard problem of evaluating agents rigorously enough to put them in front of real customers. I care about research that survives contact with production.

Before Nubank I was a Senior Staff Machine Learning Scientist at LinkedIn, where I managed the AI foundations optimization team and oversaw large-scale optimization frameworks across Feed, Ads, and Jobs. Earlier I built machine learning systems at Apple and Amazon. I studied at Carnegie Mellon and BITS Pilani.
